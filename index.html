How it works

Fifteen minutes a day is enough. Your streak and a daily reminder keep it going.

Learn an idea

A short card, a diagram or a game: put the steps in order, run a sort, trace code by hand.

Practise it

Answer, write code, type terminal commands, or play a house's task. Mistakes are explained, never punished.

Apply it for real

Use what you learnt on your own phone and accounts, then come back for the review a few days later.

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Most lessons, the first eight houses, the terminal course and the code editor are free. The Elite plan unlocks the other houses (the first task of each is free to try) and gives more AI answers a day.

Do I need to know anything first?

No. The lessons start from zero, and the Algorithms module teaches C from the first line.

Does it teach hacking?

It teaches how attacks work so you can defend against them, always with permission and the law in mind. Practice happens in safe simulations, never on real systems.

Google Play or APK?

Google Play is the easiest and updates by itself. The APK is for people without Google Play: the same lessons and labs as a separate install; check its fingerprint on the download page before installing.
