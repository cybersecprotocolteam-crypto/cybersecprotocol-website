How it works

Fifteen minutes a day is enough. Your streak and a daily review keep it going.

Learn an idea

A short card, a diagram or a game: put the steps in order, run a sort, trace code by hand.

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Most lessons, the first eight houses, the terminal course and the code editor are free. The Elite plan unlocks the other houses, more AI help and removes ads.

Do I need to know anything first?

No. The lessons start from zero, and the Algorithms module teaches C from the first line.

Does it teach hacking?

It teaches how attacks work so you can defend against them, always with permission and the law in mind. Practice happens in safe simulations, never on real systems.

Google Play or APK?

Google Play is the easiest and updates by itself. The APK is the same app for people without Google Play; check its fingerprint on the download page before installing.
