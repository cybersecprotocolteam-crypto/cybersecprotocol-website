What CyberAi does

CyberAi is the assistant inside Cybersec Protocol. Here is exactly what it does with your questions.

What it does

  • Explains lessons and answers your questions in the chat.
  • Sets the CyberAi exam in each Action House and grades it.
  • Summarises stories in Cases and answers your questions about them.
  • Writes the daily news quiz.

What it never does

  • Sends nothing to the cloud until you allow it. You can turn the cloud off at any time in Settings → CyberAi.
  • Never reads your messages, contacts, photos or the list of your apps.
  • With the cloud off, or once today’s answers are used up, your phone answers by itself and sends nothing.

What goes to Google Gemini

Only what the question needs, and only when you use it:

  • Chat: the question you type, and the lesson’s title when you ask from a lesson.
  • A story: your question, its headline, the site’s short description and a short excerpt; a summary sends the article’s text.
  • An Action House: the house’s topic and your answers.
  • The news quiz: the day’s headlines.

On Google’s free tier, Google may use this to improve its products. Never type passwords, codes or personal data. See also When the app uses the internet and the privacy policy.

Daily limits

Free: 10 cloud AI answers a day. Elite: 100. It is one shared pool for the chat and the questions about stories. Summaries in Cases are free and do not use it; a summary you already opened is free to open again. The Action Houses count apart: on Free, one CyberAi exam per house is free; another try costs coins.

Asking well

Ask one thing at a time, say what you already know, and name the lesson or the story. Ask for steps or an example if you want them. Leave out anything private.
