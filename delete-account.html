Delete your account

Last updated: 2 November 2026

You can delete your Cybersec Protocol account and the data linked to it at any time. Guests have no account: uninstalling the app erases everything.

1. From the app (fastest)

  1. Open Cybersec Protocol and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings > Account > Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in long ago, the app may ask you to sign in again first: that protects you from someone else deleting your account.

The deletion happens at once.

2. Without the app

Email cybersecprotocol.team@gmail.com from the address of your account, with the subject Delete my account. We confirm by email and delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner. We may ask you to confirm that the address is yours.

3. What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email, name, the Google sign-in link if you used one).
  • Your wallet in our database: coins, freezes, unlocked themes, purchase codes and the Supporter badge.
  • Your recovery-phrase record, your invite code, your league nickname and scores, and the reactions and gifts linked to your account.
  • On the phone: progress, settings, saved articles, code drafts and chat history, when the app deletes the account (or when you uninstall it).

4. What may be kept

  • Lemon Squeezy keeps the records of your payments, as the law requires them to (usually for several years for tax purposes). Ask them through your receipt to delete what they are allowed to delete.
  • Our purchase server keeps only a one-way fingerprint (SHA-256) of each licence key with anonymous fingerprints of the accounts that redeemed it, so a key cannot be redeemed twice. These cannot be linked back to you once your account is gone, and they end with the licence.
  • Paid plans are not cancelled by deleting the account: cancel them from your Lemon Squeezy receipt first.
