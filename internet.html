When the app uses the internet

Most of Cybersec Protocol works with no connection. A few features contact other services. Here is every one of them, in plain words.

The app has no analytics, and we do not sell or share your personal data. The services below see only what is written here. For the legal wording, see the privacy policy.

FeatureNeeds the internet?Who it contactsWhat is sentHow to switch it off
Lessons, quizzes, hands-on games, the terminal, the code editor, house tasksNo. They work offline.
Cases: the news browserYesThe news sites, security feeds (NVD and others) and the pictures of storiesA normal web request: your phone’s IP address and device type. No account, name or email.Do not open Cases. Turn off the daily notification if you do not want a daily news request. Turn off “Story pictures” in Settings → Cases to stop the photos from loading.
Searching in CasesYesNVD and DuckDuckGoThe words you search for.Do not search.
Videos in lessons and CasesYesYouTube (Google), through its privacy-enhanced playerA request to play the video.Do not press play.
CyberAi in the cloud (chat, story questions, summaries, house exams, the news quiz)Yes, and only after you allow itGoogle (the Gemini service)The chat: your question, and the lesson title when you ask from a lesson. A story: your question, its headline, the site’s short description and an excerpt; a summary sends the article text. A house: its topic and your answers. The news quiz: the day’s headlines.Choose “CyberAi Offline” in the chat, or settings → CyberAi → turn the cloud off. Offline answers stay on your phone.
Saving your chats to your account (optional)Yes, only if you turn it onGoogle Firebase (database)Your CyberAi chats: your questions and its answers. Only you can read them.It is off by default. Settings → CyberAi → “Save chats to my account”: turning it off deletes them from your account (they stay on your phone).
Sign-in and your accountYes, if you make an accountGoogle Firebase (sign-in and database)Your email, a nickname, your coins and plan, and your reactions. Guests do not need an account.Use the app as a guest, or delete your account in Settings (or on the delete-account page).

What no feature does

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, call log or SMS. Your lesson progress, your code and your phone checks stay on your phone.
