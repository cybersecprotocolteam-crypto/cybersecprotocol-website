How to Learn Cybersecurity

Intro to Cybersecurity · about 6 minutes

1. Build the foundations first

People who work in security agree on the order: networking (how the internet works), operating systems (Linux and Windows) and one programming language (Python is the usual first choice). Tools change every year; these foundations do not.

2. Practise a little every day

Reading is not enough: security is learned by doing.

  • Use practice labs made for learning (you will find some in the next lesson).
  • Type the commands yourself on the Terminal path of this app.
  • Keep notes of what you learn: a notebook or a notes app is enough.
  • Twenty minutes a day beats five hours once a month. Your streak is there to help.

Warning

Only test systems you own, or that you have written permission to test. Scanning or attacking someone else's network, website or account is illegal in most countries, even "just to see". Practice labs exist so that you never have to.

3. Habits of good learners

  • Ask why, not only how: understand what each attack takes advantage of.
  • Follow the news in the Cases tab, and link each story to a lesson.
  • Join a community: forums and Discord groups help when you are stuck.
  • Build small projects: a password checker, a script that sorts logs, a scan of your own home network.
  • Be patient: everyone starts as a beginner, and being confused is part of learning.

Key Takeaway

Foundations first, practice every day (legally), and keep notes. Next: free courses and practice platforms that the community recommends.
