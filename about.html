About Cybersec Protocol

Most people are told to "be careful online" and never shown how. Cybersec Protocol exists to change that: to teach how attacks really work, how to defend against them, and how to write the code that does it, in a way that fits in a few minutes a day.

Who we are

Cybersec Protocol is an independent app, designed and built in Algeria by a small team of learners turned makers. We are not backed by an advertising company and we do not sell data. The app is paid for by people who choose a plan, and by optional ads in the free version.

What we believe

  • Learning is doing. Every idea comes with something to try: a question, a game, code to run or a command to type.
  • Security is for everyone. The basics (a screen lock, updates, a password manager, two-step sign-in) are free in the app, for everyone, forever.
  • Fair rewards. Coins come from learning. A wrong answer never costs a coin, and nothing random is sold for money.
  • Privacy first. Your code, your progress and your phone checks stay on your phone. Guests need no account at all.
  • Ethics always. We teach attacks so people can defend. Practising on systems you do not own or have no written permission to test is illegal, and the app says so.

What is inside

Fifteen learning modules (from network security to algorithms in C), sixteen houses on the 3D Cyber Trail with games and exams, real phone-protection steps, a nine-unit terminal course, a code playground, a live threat browser and an optional AI coach. The app is available in English, Arabic, French and Spanish.

Talk to us

Ideas, bugs or questions: write to cybersecprotocol.team@gmail.com or join the Discord community. Found a security problem? See how to report it.
